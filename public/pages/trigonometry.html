📐 Trigonometry

Basic & Reciprocal Identities

▾
Reciprocal Must Know
sinθ = 1/cosecθ, cosθ = 1/secθ, tanθ = 1/cotθ
Each ratio is the reciprocal of another.
Quotient
tanθ = sinθ/cosθ, cotθ = cosθ/sinθ
Relates tan/cot to sin and cos.

Pythagorean Identities

▾
Core Must Know
sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
Foundation identity — derive the other two from this.
Secant form
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ
Divide core identity by cos²θ.
Cosecant form
1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ
Divide core identity by sin²θ.
🎯 Shortcut: remember "1+tan²=sec², 1+cot²=cosec²" as a pair.

Compound & Double Angle

▾
sin(A±B) Must Know
sin(A±B) = sinA cosB ± cosA sinB
Core compound-angle expansion for sine.
cos(A±B) Must Know
cos(A±B) = cosA cosB ∓ sinA sinB
Sign flips opposite to the sine version.
tan(A±B)
tan(A±B) = (tanA ± tanB)/(1 ∓ tanA tanB)
Divide sin(A±B) by cos(A±B).
Double angle Must Know
sin2θ = 2sinθcosθ, cos2θ = 1−2sin²θ
cos2θ also = 2cos²θ−1 = cos²θ−sin²θ.
🎯 Common mistake: forgetting cos2θ has 3 equivalent forms.

General Solutions

▾
sinθ = sinα Must Know
θ = nπ + (−1)ⁿα, n ∈ ℤ
General solution family for sine equality.
cosθ = cosα
θ = 2nπ ± α, n ∈ ℤ
General solution family for cosine equality.
tanθ = tanα
θ = nπ + α, n ∈ ℤ
Simplest period since tan repeats every π.

Properties of Triangles

▾
Sine Rule Must Know
a/sinA = b/sinB = c/sinC = 2R
R is the circumradius of the triangle.
Cosine Rule Must Know
a² = b² + c² − 2bc·cosA
Use when two sides and included angle are known.
Area of triangle
Δ = ½ ab sinC
Handy when two sides and included angle are given.
